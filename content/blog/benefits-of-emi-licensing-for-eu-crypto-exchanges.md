In this presentation, we look into why crypto exchanges might want to consider becoming regulated Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs). This appears to be a particularly interesting strategy in order to gain greater control over EUR rails.

The key insights you will find in this presentation are:

1️⃣ **Enhanced Credibility**: EMI licensing strengthens industry standing, establishing trust with EU regulators and global financial entities.

2️⃣ **Operational Independence**: Offers capabilities like issuing prepaid/debit cards, handling cross-border money transfers, and direct online fiat payment processing.

3️⃣ **Reduced Reliance on Banks**: Eases the management of operational bank accounts, addressing the challenge of maintaining banking relationships.

4️⃣ **Risk Reduction & Improved Client Service**s: Simplifies fiat conversion, potentially offering individual EUR IBANs to users, enhancing the overall user experience.

5️⃣ **Strategic Advantages**: Ensures regulatory compliance, provides market differentiation, and fosters customer trust and loyalty. It also opens avenues for innovation and long-term sustainability in a changing regulatory landscape.
