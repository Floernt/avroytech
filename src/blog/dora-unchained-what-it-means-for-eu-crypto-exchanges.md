We delve into the EU Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) and its implications for EU crypto exchanges (CASPs) that want to stay afloat of this latest regulatory “*tsunami*”

**Key insights include:**

1️⃣ **DORA’s Scope:** Targets cybersecurity resilience of all EU regulated financial entities, including CASPs. It becomes effective in Jan 2025, shortly after MiCA.

2️⃣ **DORA’s Mandates:** CASPs must manage ICT risks, report significant incidents to regulators, and conduct regular resilience testing.

3️⃣ **Third-party risks:** crucially, a focus on managing ICT third-party providers, particularly for “critical” and “important” business functions like wallet infrastructure, Blockchain analytics, Staking-as-a-Service, Travel rule providers, and KYC services.

4️⃣ **Impact of DORA:** Often overshadowed by MiCA, DORA presents substantial challenges and compliance costs for CASPs.

**Final Thoughts:** The MiCA/DORA era may lead to market consolidation, as not all CASPs will survive the coming “purge”.  But it will also offer opportunities for resilient CASPs. EU Regulation akin to traditional financial institutions should hopefully bring much-needed trust and interest in the crypto sector!
